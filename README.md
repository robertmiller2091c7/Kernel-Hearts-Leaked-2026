## 🕹️ About

Kernel Hearts is a multiplayer co-op roguelike action RPG about the M.A.H.O.U Unit, a squad of android magical girls climbing the Tower of Babel to destroy God and save a world drowning in ashes. This unofficial Windows build surfaced ahead of the scheduled September 17, 2026 release and remains raw, unfinished, and unpolished. It is not affiliated with Ephemera Games or Whitethorn Games.

Built for players who want anime-styled action RPG combat, punishing roguelike runs, and chaotic co-op with an unfinished underground edge.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- ⚔️ **Fast-Paced Combat** — Chain attacks, spells, movement, and unique powers into devastating combos.
- 👥 **Four-Player Co-Op** — Fight alone or team up with up to three allies against the Tower's angels.
- 🗼 **Climb Babel** — Push through dangerous domains and face powerful guardians at each level.
- 💾 **Unit Customization** — Equip chips to your M.A.H.O.U Unit and reshape your build between expeditions.
- ✨ **Magical Transformation** — Break the Heavenly Curse and temporarily unleash your unit's true form.
- 💬 **Bonds and Allies** — Return to humanity's refuge, meet its inhabitants, and deepen relationships through gifts.
